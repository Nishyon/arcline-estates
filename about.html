Our story

We started with one belief: property decisions are too important to be rushed or oversold. That belief still guides every deal we close.

01Who we are

An advisory firm, not a listing portal.

Arcline Estates is a team of 140 advisors, lawyers, valuers and project managers across six cities. We represent buyers, tenants and developers — and we tell each of them the truth about price.

Our commercial practice has leased more than 6.8 million sq.ft of office space to companies from 12-person start-ups to Fortune 500 tenants. In 2025 we opened our own flagship building, Arcline One, in GIFT City.
