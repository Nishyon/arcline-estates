FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge buyers a brokerage fee?

For new-launch projects, no — the developer pays our fee. For resale homes we charge 1% of the transaction value, and 1 month's rent for residential leases. Commercial fees are agreed in writing before any site visit.

Can NRIs buy through Arcline remotely?

Yes. Our NRI desk handles video walkthroughs, power-of-attorney drafting, NRE/NRO account payments and registration — you only need to travel if you want to.

How do you verify a property before listing it?

Our legal team checks the title chain for 30 years, encumbrance certificates, approved plans, occupancy certificate and RERA registration. Listings that fail any check are not published.

How quickly can my company move into an office?

Managed and plug-and-play offices are typically ready in 3–5 weeks. A bare-shell fit-out takes 10–16 weeks depending on size and specification.

Can you help me get a home loan?

Our loan desk works with eleven banks and housing-finance companies. We compare offers, prepare your file and track sanction and disbursement for you.
